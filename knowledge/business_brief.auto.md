# InterviewSarthi: auto brief
Generated 10 November 2026 from 40 pages of https://interviewsarthi.com.

## One line
InterviewSarthi provides three privacy-focused job search tools for Indian job seekers: ApplySarthi for job matching and form filling, Prep Sarthi for AI mock interviews, and Live Sarthi for live CV-grounded call assistance on Windows.

## What it does, in detail
InterviewSarthi offers three distinct applications addressing different stages of the job search process:

*   **ApplySarthi**: Aggregates job postings from 21 sources (including LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Foundit, Shine, Internshala, and 754 company career boards) into a single search workspace. It ranks postings against the user's CV, generates tailored CVs and cover letters, and autofills application forms in Google Chrome.
*   **Prep Sarthi**: A browser-based voice application for spoken AI mock interviews. It generates practice questions based on the user's CV and target job description, asks follow-up questions on weak answers, and provides feedback.
*   **Live Sarthi**: A Windows desktop application that listens to live call audio during an interview and displays resume-grounded answer suggestions on screen in real time (in English, Hindi, or Hinglish) without joining the meeting as a bot.

Note on page discrepancies: The homepage states Live Sarthi answers start appearing in "about 1 second", whereas the About page states replies start in "about 1.5 seconds under suitable conditions". Both pages note that speed varies.

## How it works (technology, privacy, requirements)
*   **AI Engine & Keys**: Powered by Google Gemini. Users supply their own free Google Gemini API key for paid passes, Live Sarthi, and ongoing ApplySarthi document tailoring (the first tailored CV is free on the platform). Google's API limits and charges are separate from Sarthi passes.
*   **Live Sarthi Requirements & Privacy**: Requires Windows 10 (version 2004+) or Windows 11, a microphone, and a Gemini key. The answer window hides itself from supported Windows screen capture methods. No bot joins the call. Free trial provides 30 total minutes per device.
*   **Prep Sarthi Requirements**: Requires a web browser and microphone on a phone or desktop. The 7-minute free demo requires no sign-up or API key. The paid 30-day pass uses Google sign-in and the user's Gemini key.
*   **ApplySarthi Requirements & Privacy**: Runs in Chrome on desktop for form autofill. CV matching requires signing in and uploading a PDF or Word document (up to 5 MB). The free ATS Resume Checker processes files entirely within the local browser tab using PDF.js and Mammoth; CVs are never uploaded, stored, or recorded. Autofill stops for user review before submission by default (optional Autopilot submit mode must be manually enabled).

## Frequently asked questions (as answered on the site)
*   **Does Live Sarthi appear in a screen share?**
    It hides its answer panel from supported Windows screen capture on Windows 10 (2004+) and Windows 11. It does not cover every recording method; users must test their setup and follow interview rules.
*   **How fast does Live Sarthi show answers?**
    Answers start appearing in about 1 second (or 1.5 seconds under suitable conditions). Start times vary depending on network, model, and question.
*   **Is ApplySarthi really free?**
    Yes. ApplySarthi is always free with no paid plan. Job browsing and the first tailored CV require no API key; ongoing tailoring uses your own free Google Gemini key.
*   **Does ApplySarthi automatically submit applications?**
    No. By default, it fills forms in your browser for review before submitting. Optional Autopilot submission exists but is off by default.
*   **Can I use Live Sarthi on a Mac?**
    No. Live Sarthi is Windows-only (Windows 10/11).
